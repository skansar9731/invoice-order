Privacy Policy

MH AUTO — AI Handwritten Order to Busy Entry Sheet

Last updated: 12 September 2026

MH AUTO is a business utility for converting customer handwritten spare-parts orders into structured order information and generating Busy entry PDF/Excel files.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the application, the app may process:

  • Customer order information that you enter or upload, including handwritten order images.
  • Order fields such as customer name, created-by and checked-by information that you enter.
  • Product, stock, unit, MRP, rack and part information loaded into the application.
  • Google account information needed to authorize Google Drive access, such as the signed-in account identity.
  • Generated PDF and Excel sales-order documents.

2. How Handwritten Images Are Processed

When AI extraction is enabled, an uploaded order image is sent from the application to the application's Netlify serverless endpoint for handwriting extraction. That service sends the image to Google Gemini for processing and receives structured order text and quantities.

We use the image only for the order-extraction workflow described above. Do not upload sensitive information that is not necessary for processing an order.

3. Google Drive

If you choose to use the Google Drive export feature, the app asks you to sign in with Google and authorize the application to work with the Drive files/folders selected for this app.

Generated sales-order PDF and Excel files may be uploaded to the Google Drive folder you authorize, such as the MH SALES ORDER folder and its month folders. The files remain under the control of your Google account and are subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

The app does not request unrestricted access to your entire Google Drive. It uses Google's limited drive.file scope for the Drive integration.

4. Local Storage and Offline Data

The application is designed as a local-first PWA. Product/master data and application settings may be stored in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB so that the app can continue working locally and offline where supported.

Clearing browser site data or using another browser/device may remove locally stored application data.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the app's functions, including order extraction, product matching, order preparation, PDF/Excel generation, and Google Drive export when requested by the user.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers that are required to provide functionality that you explicitly use, including Google Gemini for handwriting extraction and Google Drive for files you choose to save there.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect application data. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. You should avoid uploading information that you are not authorized to share.

8. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to use AI handwriting extraction.
  • You can choose whether to authorize Google Drive export.
  • You can remove Google Drive access from your Google Account at any time through your Google account permissions.
  • You can clear browser site data to remove locally stored application data from that browser.

9. Third-Party Services

The application may use Google services for authentication, Drive file storage, and AI processing. Those services are governed by their own policies and terms.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is a business utility and is not intended for children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
